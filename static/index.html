About This Build

TRADEXO is a paper/simulated research engine — no real broker order placement is executed (SmartAPI stream is used read-only for live market data & tick-rule delta analysis). All quantitative rules, thresholds, and scoring logic are identical across desktop and mobile.

RULES Priority Ranking & Gap Prediction — Exactly How The Engine Scores

Our quantitative engine predicts overnight gap movements and ranks Indian NSE F&O stocks using 5 institutional pillars:

🟢 CALL (CE) vs 🔴 PUT (PE) Option Signals

CALL (CE) represents overnight long breakout setups (BTST Buy). PUT (PE) represents overnight short distribution setups (STBT Sell).

📈 Autonomous Background Scanning Schedule & 5-Step Closing Sequence

9:15 AM – 2:30 PM: 5-minute background scanning.
2:30 PM – 3:14 PM (Power Hour): High-frequency scanning pass.
3:14 PM Candidate Snapshot: Candidate setup candidates are frozen prior to market closing sequence.
3:25 PM Auto-Lock Snapshot: 3:25 PM candidate setup snapshot automatically compiled.
3:30 PM Official Market Bell Lock: Picks officially locked at market close & persisted to Signal Journal & Trade History.
3:30 PM – 9:15 AM Next Day (Off-Market): Scanning freezes and serves the frozen 3:30 PM BTST pick snapshot.

🎯 9:15-9:17 AM Auto-Analyse & Accuracy Auto-Upgrade Engine

Every morning in a tight 9:15-9:17 AM window, the system fetches the real opening prices, compares Predicted Gap % vs Actual Gap % for yesterday's locked picks, records variance error, and updates win rate / accuracy metrics — once a day, not repeatedly.

🧪 Fundamental & News Quality Gates

Fundamentals (P/E, ROE, debt/equity, earnings growth) and stock-specific news are checked as quality gates on top of the technical setup — they can only cap conviction (e.g. P1 → P2) when the business or headlines look risky, never manufacture it. Both are shown in each pick's depth-analysis.

⚙️ Auto-Improving Pillar Weights

Once there are 30+ graded predictions, the system recomputes each of the 5 technical pillars' real hit rate and nudges its scoring weight toward that — capped at ±15%/day so one lucky or unlucky streak can't swing live scoring. Check /api/validation for the full history.

📰 News Coverage, Budget-Aware

Stocks News covers every F&O stock (the full option-chain universe). A background job refreshes it up to 3x/day within CurrentsAPI's daily limit — your page views never call the API directly, so any number of people can browse News at zero extra cost.
